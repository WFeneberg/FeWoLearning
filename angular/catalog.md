## Beginner (001–035) — components & DI
Standalone components, interpolation & binding, `@Input`/`@Output`, `@if`/`@for`
control flow, event binding, services & `providedIn`, dependency injection, signals
(`signal`/`computed`), template refs, pipes (built-in), lifecycle hooks.

## Intermediate (036–070) — reactive patterns
Reactive forms (`FormGroup`/`FormControl`/validators), `HttpClient` + testing
(`HttpTestingController`), RxJS operators (`map`/`switchMap`/`debounceTime`),
`AsyncPipe`, custom pipes, structural directives, content projection (`ng-content`),
`inject()` function, route params, `effect()`, `linkedSignal`.

## Advanced (071–090) — architecture & performance
Router guards & resolvers, lazy-loaded routes, state with signals or NgRx,
`OnPush` change detection, `ChangeDetectorRef`, interceptors, custom form controls
(`ControlValueAccessor`), dynamic component loading, `@defer` blocks, directive
composition, testing async flows with `fakeAsync`/`tick`.

## Expert (091–100) — systems & design
A feature-sliced architecture, a typed data-access layer, a signal-based store
library, an SSR/hydration-aware component, a schema-driven form renderer, a design-
system component with a11y, a plugin/extension point via injection tokens, a
WebSocket live-data service, a micro-frontend shell, an internationalization layer.
